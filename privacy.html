Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and ISO/IEC 27001

1. Data Privacy Principles

At NEXVORA TECHNOLOGIES, we believe data privacy is a foundational engineering requirement. We do not sell, monetize, or broker personal or corporate information. Any information collected through our website, project scope estimators, or client portals is strictly utilized to deliver contractual engineering services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information knowingly provided by clients and candidates:

  • Project Inquiries & Quotes: Full name, corporate email address, telephone number, technical requirements, and estimated project budgets.
  • Job Applications: Contact details, employment history, portfolio links, and uploaded resumes.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized server logs necessary to ensure operational stability, DDOS prevention, and network security.

3. Offline & Zero Third-Party Tracker Architecture

Our core website is engineered to operate without external CDNs or third-party behavioral trackers. We do not embed surveillance pixels, Google Analytics tracking scripts, or ad exchange telemetry. All state management for local demonstration features is isolated inside your browser's private localStorage.

4. Data Encryption & Security Standards

All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption with modern forward secrecy cipher suites. Backend database records are stored with AES-256 encryption at rest, protected by strict firewall rules and role-based access control (RBAC).

5. Your Legal Rights

You have the right to request a complete export of your personal data or demand its immediate permanent deletion from our records. To exercise these rights, contact company ownership directly at arifkhankkhan002@gmail.com.
